An NRI can't buy agricultural land, plantation property or a farm house in India under the general rules. It needs specific approval from the Reserve Bank of India. Since most land for sale in Coorg is agricultural or coffee land, this affects most listings.

## In short

| Situation | NRI |
|---|---|
| Buy agricultural land, plantation or farm house | Needs specific RBI approval |
| Buy residential or commercial property | Allowed under the general rules |
| Inherit agricultural land | Allowed |
| Bought while living in India, then moved abroad | Can keep it |

## What it means for Coorg

Check early whether the land is agricultural, plantation or converted. Your lawyer can confirm from its records.

The 2020 Karnataka change that let non-farmers buy agricultural land applies to people living in India. It doesn't change the NRI rules, which are central.

## Paying

NRIs pay through normal banking channels: money sent to India, or an NRE, FCNR(B) or NRO account. Not in cash or foreign currency notes.

## FAQs

### Can an NRI buy agricultural land in Coorg?
Not under the general rules. It needs specific approval from the Reserve Bank of India.

### Can an NRI inherit land in Coorg?
Yes.

### I bought land while living in India. Can I keep it if I move abroad?
Yes. You can continue to hold it.

### Can an NRI buy a residential property in Coorg?
Yes. Residential and commercial property are allowed under the general rules.
