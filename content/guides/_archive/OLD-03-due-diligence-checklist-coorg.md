Before you pay any token amount for land in Coorg, check the RTC and mutation register on Bhoomi, the encumbrance certificate on Kaveri, the survey sketch, the conversion order if you plan to build, and the chain of sale deeds. Then walk the boundary, the approach road and the slope with the survey sketch in hand. You can do most of the paperwork checks online in an evening. The ground checks need a site visit and ideally a licensed surveyor.

I'm selling [land in Galibeedu](https://landincoorg.in/). This is the list I would want a buyer to work through, on our land or anyone else's.

## The checklist

| # | Check | What to look for | Where | Online? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | RTC (pahani) | Owner's name matches the seller, survey number, extent, land type, any liabilities or cases recorded | [Bhoomi](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service2/): View RTC and MR | Yes |
| 2 | Mutation register (MR) | How the seller came to own it, and whether any mutation is pending | Bhoomi: MR and Mutation Status | Yes |
| 3 | Encumbrance certificate (EC) | Registered mortgages, earlier sales, agreements | [Kaveri](https://kaveri.karnataka.gov.in), or the Sub-Registrar's office | Yes, with a login |
| 4 | Survey sketch and revenue map | Shape and boundaries of the survey number, and its sub-divisions (hissa) | Bhoomi: Survey Documents and [Revenue Maps](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/service3) | Yes |
| 5 | Khata extract | Revenue account entry for the land | Bhoomi: View Khatha Extract | Yes |
| 6 | Conversion order (if you will build) | Survey number and area match the land you're shown; permitted use | Bhoomi: Land Conversion Services, "Download final conversion orders" | Yes |
| 7 | Title chain | Each earlier sale deed, going back as far as your lawyer advises | Seller, plus certified copies from the Sub-Registrar | Partly |
| 8 | Boundary on the ground | Boundary stones match the sketch | On site, with a licensed surveyor | No |
| 9 | Approach road | Recorded right of way, usable in the monsoon | On site, plus revenue map | Partly |
| 10 | Forest edge | Whether any boundary touches notified or deemed forest | Forest department, Madikeri | No |
| 11 | Slope and drainage | Cut slopes, cracks, water paths, signs of past slips | On site, in the monsoon if you can | No |

## 1 to 5: the records on Bhoomi

The state's land records portal, [Bhoomi](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/), lets you look up any survey number by district, taluk, hobli and village. For land in Galibeedu, the district is Kodagu and the taluk is Madikeri. From the same portal you can view the RTC and mutation register, the khata extract, survey documents and revenue maps.

Things to compare across the records:

- The owner's name on the RTC should match the person selling. If several people own it jointly, every owner has to sign the sale.
- The extent on the RTC should match what you're being sold. If the seller quotes a figure from a private survey drawing, ask why it differs from the RTC.
- Check the RTC for recorded loans, court cases or government claims.
- The mutation register shows how ownership changed hands. A mutation that is still pending is a reason to wait.

The state's survey app, [Dishaank](https://kgis.ksrsac.in/kgisdocuments/KGIS2021/Dishaank_Help%20Document.pdf), shows survey numbers on a map using your phone's GPS. Its own user manual says its boundaries are not to be used for any legal purpose. Use it to find the land, not to settle a boundary.

## 3: Encumbrance certificate

The EC lists registered transactions on the property, such as sales, mortgages and agreements. Get it from [Kaveri](https://kaveri.karnataka.gov.in), the Department of Stamps and Registration's online portal, or from the Sub-Registrar's office. Ask your lawyer how many years to cover. It should go back at least as far as the title chain you're checking.

## 6: Conversion, if you plan to build

You cannot build a house on agricultural land until it has been converted (diverted) under section 95 of the Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964. If the seller says the land is converted, download the final conversion order from Bhoomi and check its survey number, area and permitted use against the land. The process is in [converting agricultural land in Kodagu](/guides/convert-agricultural-land-kodagu/).

## 7: Title chain and tenure

Your lawyer should read every sale deed in the chain and confirm each seller had the right to sell. Coorg has local land tenures, such as jamma and bane, that you won't see elsewhere in Karnataka. I haven't found an official source I trust that explains how they affect a sale today, so ask your lawyer to check the tenure shown on the RTC. My notes are in [jamma and bane land in Coorg](/guides/jamma-bane-land-coorg/).

If you are an NRI, check first whether you can buy agricultural land at all. See [can a non-farmer buy agricultural land in Karnataka](/guides/non-farmer-buy-agricultural-land-karnataka/).

## 8 to 11: on the ground

- **Boundary.** Walk it with the survey sketch. A licensed surveyor can check the stones against the sketch.
- **Approach road.** Make sure the road to the gate is a recorded right of way, not a track across someone else's land, and that you can use it in July.
- **Forest edge.** Ask the forest department in Madikeri whether the survey number is in, or borders, notified or deemed forest. In May 2026, [The Hindu reported](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Mangalore/joint-survey-of-deemed-forest-60-65-complete-says-karnataka-forest-minister-eshwar-khandre/article71021331.ece) that Karnataka's joint survey of deemed forest was 60 to 65% complete, so records may still change.
- **Slope.** The [Kodagu District Disaster Management Plan](https://kodagu.nic.in/document/kodagu-disaster-management/) says the district's terrain is sensitive, that changes in land use can cause landslides or slope failures, and that slopes along the highway are at significant risk, mainly from rain-induced slope failures. I haven't found an official landslide map detailed enough to show individual survey numbers. If the land is steep or below a road cut, get a geotechnical engineer to look at it. More in [landslide risk when buying land in Kodagu](/guides/kodagu-landslide-risk-land/).

## FAQs

### Where can I check the RTC for land in Coorg?
On Bhoomi, the state's land records portal at landrecords.karnataka.gov.in. Choose Kodagu, then the taluk, hobli, village and survey number.

### Where do I get an encumbrance certificate for land in Kodagu?
From Kaveri, the Department of Stamps and Registration's online portal, after you register a login, or from the Sub-Registrar's office for the area.

### Can I rely on Dishaank for the land boundary?
No. Dishaank's own user manual says the boundaries it shows are not for any legal purpose. Use a licensed surveyor and the official survey sketch.

### How do I know if land in Coorg is near forest?
Ask the forest department in Madikeri whether the survey number is in, or borders, notified or deemed forest. Karnataka's joint survey of deemed forest was still under way in 2026.

### Is there an official landslide map for my survey number?
I haven't found one detailed enough to show individual survey numbers. The district disaster plan describes the risk in general terms. For steep land, get a geotechnical engineer's opinion.

### Which office registers land sales in Madikeri taluk?
The Sub-Registrar Office, Madikeri, Room 15, 1st Floor, Mini Vidhanasoudha, Madikeri.
