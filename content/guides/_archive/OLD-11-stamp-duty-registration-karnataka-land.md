On a land sale deed in Karnataka the stamp duty is 5% of the market value plus surcharge and additional duty, and the registration fee is 2% of the market value. Those are the figures the [Department of Stamps and Registration](https://igr.karnataka.gov.in/page/Stamp+Duty+and+Registration+Fees/en) publishes for a conveyance, and I have quoted them as published rather than added up the surcharge myself.

These two charges sit on top of the price you agree with the seller, so they matter when you work out what land near Madikeri actually costs you. They apply to [our own land in Galibeedu](https://landincoorg.in/) the same as any other, and we're open about that.

## The two charges

| Charge | Rate (as published) | On what |
|---|---|---|
| Stamp duty on a conveyance (sale deed) | 5% + surcharge + additional duty | market value of the land |
| Registration fee | 2% | market value of the land |

The source is the Department of Stamps and Registration's "Stamp Duty and Registration Fees" page.

## What "market value" means, and why it isn't your price

Stamp duty is charged on the market value of the land, which the registration department determines, not on the price you and the seller agree. The department publishes [guidance values](https://igr.karnataka.gov.in/page/Stamp+Duty+and+Registration+Fees/en), the minimum values it applies by area. If your agreed price is below the guidance value, duty is still calculated on the guidance value.

## What I could not verify

- **The surcharge and additional duty in percentage terms.** The department's page states "surcharge" and "additional duty" but does not put a percentage on either in the table I read. So I have not added them to the 5%. Use the calculator on the [Kaveri portal](https://kaverionline.karnataka.gov.in/landing-page), or ask the Sub-Registrar's office, for the exact total on your deed.

## Why this matters before you agree a price

Stamp duty and registration together are a large, unavoidable cost that follows the market value, so it is the same for a negotiable asking price and a firm one. When a seller says "negotiable", the stamp duty does not come down with the price below the guidance value. Budget for duty on the higher of the two.

## FAQs

### What is the stamp duty on land in Karnataka?
5% of the market value, plus surcharge and additional duty, for a conveyance (sale deed), according to the Department of Stamps and Registration.

### What is the registration fee?
2% of the market value.

### Is stamp duty on the sale price or the guidance value?
On the market value, which the registration department determines. If the agreed price is below the guidance value, duty is calculated on the guidance value.

### Can I calculate the exact duty myself?
Use the Kaveri portal's calculator or ask the Sub-Registrar's office. The department's published table states the surcharge and additional duty without percentages, so I could not add them up here.

### Where do I register a land sale in Madikeri taluk?
At the Sub-Registrar Office, Madikeri, Room 15, 1st Floor, Mini Vidhanasoudha.
