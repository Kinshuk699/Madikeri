## What the table shows

- **Size changes the per-acre price a lot.** The two largest parcels, 16 and 53 acres, were both listed at ₹60 lakh per acre. Two of the three plots under half an acre were listed above ₹5 crore per acre.
- **What is on the land matters.** The list includes a coffee estate, a farmhouse and a plot with an existing building. One 99acres plot in Galibeedu says it has an old house and a BSNL tower on it.
- **The same area can vary widely.** The six Galibeedu listings ranged from ₹60 lakh to ₹5.5 crore per acre.
- **Who is selling differs.** 99acres and MagicBricks marked most of these as owner listings. 1acre.in marked its four as "Facilitated". One MagicBricks plot appeared twice, once as an owner listing and once from an agent, and I counted it once.

## Asking prices are not sale prices

A seller can list at any price, so treat this table as a starting point for negotiation, not a valuation. Registered sale values are not published listing by listing. For stamp duty, the state uses guidance values, which it publishes through the [Department of Stamps and Registration](https://igr.karnataka.gov.in/page/Stamp+Duty+and+Registration+Fees/en). See the [stamp duty guide](/guides/stamp-duty-registration-karnataka-land/) for how that works.

## FAQs

### What is the price of land per acre in Madikeri?
In public listings checked on 29 and 30 October 2026, asking prices around Madikeri ran from about ₹37 lakh to ₹5.5 crore per acre, with a median of about ₹1.5 crore. These are asking prices, not sale prices.

### What do land prices look like in Galibeedu?
Six Galibeedu listings in October 2026 asked between ₹60 lakh and ₹5.5 crore per acre, with a median of about ₹2.13 crore.

### Are these sale prices?
No. They are what sellers listed on public portals. Registered sale values aren't published listing by listing.

### Why do small plots cost so much more per acre?
In this month's listings, plots under half an acre were often near the top of the range, and the largest parcels were near the bottom. Road frontage, buildings on the land and conversion status also affect the price.
