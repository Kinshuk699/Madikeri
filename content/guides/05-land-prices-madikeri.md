## What drives the spread

- Size. The largest parcels (16 and 53 acres) were listed at ₹60 lakh per acre. Small plots under half an acre were often above ₹5 crore per acre.
- What's on the land. Coffee, a farmhouse or an existing building all change the price.
- Road access and whether the land is converted.
