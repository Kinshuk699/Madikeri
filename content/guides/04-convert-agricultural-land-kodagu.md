To build a house on agricultural land in Kodagu, you first need it converted for non-agricultural use. You apply to the Deputy Commissioner of Kodagu, and since 2023 the law sets short deadlines for a decision.

## Who approves it

The Deputy Commissioner of Kodagu, in Madikeri. The application goes in with an affidavit.

## How long it takes

| Situation | Time the law allows |
|---|---|
| The use matches a published Master Plan for that land | 7 days |
| No published Master Plan, or the land is outside a planning area | 30 days |

If the office doesn't decide within the time allowed, the law treats the application as approved.

## Cost

There is a government conversion fee, which depends on the land. Ask the Deputy Commissioner's office or a lawyer for the figure for a specific survey number.

## What to ask a seller

- Is the land converted, or still agricultural?
- If converted, can I see the conversion order? Does its survey number and area match the land?
- If not converted, have you looked into conversion?

## FAQs

### How long does land conversion take in Karnataka?
Since 2023, 7 days where the use matches a published Master Plan, and 30 days otherwise, with approval treated as given if the office misses the deadline.

### Who approves land conversion in Kodagu?
The Deputy Commissioner of Kodagu.

### Can I build on agricultural land without converting it?
No. It has to be converted first.

### How much does conversion cost?
It depends on the land. Ask the Deputy Commissioner's office for the fee for the specific survey number.
