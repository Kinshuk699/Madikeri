Galibeedu is a hill area in Madikeri taluk, Kodagu (Coorg), about 13.7 km by road from the Madikeri KSRTC bus stand, or around 36 minutes. Club Mahindra Madikeri is 9.7 km away and Taj Madikeri Resort & Spa is 13.1 km away, both by road.

I measured these distances on Google Maps on 29 October 2026, starting from the centre of [the land we are selling](https://landincoorg.in/) in Galibeedu (12.4964° N, 75.7023° E). If you start from another part of Galibeedu, your numbers will be a little different.

## How far is Galibeedu from…

| To | By road | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Club Mahindra Madikeri | 9.7 km | about 25 min |
| Taj Madikeri Resort & Spa | 13.1 km | about 36 min |
| Madikeri KSRTC bus stand | 13.7 km | about 36 min |

| Mangaluru International Airport | 157 km | about 3 h 50 min |
| Bengaluru | 264 km | about 5 h 30 min (via NH 275) |

The Club Mahindra and Taj routes go via Kalooru Road. Times are what Google Maps estimated on a weekday in October and will vary with traffic and weather.

The district plan says Madikeri taluk, in the west of the district, gets the most rain, and rainfall falls off as you go east. Our land in Galibeedu sits at about 1,040 m, going by its site survey.

## Is Galibeedu a good place to buy land?

It depends on what you want. In its favour: it is close to Madikeri and to established resorts, and it sits high in the hills. Things to check for yourself:

- **The monsoon road.** Drive the last few kilometres between June and September.
- **Slope and drainage.** The district disaster plan says land-use change and road cutting on Kodagu's slopes can cause landslides. See [landslide risk when buying land in Kodagu](/guides/kodagu-landslide-risk-land/).
- **Mobile signal and power.** I haven't found an official coverage map for Galibeedu. Test your own network on site.
- **Price.** Six Galibeedu listings in October 2026 asked between ₹60 lakh and ₹5.5 crore per acre. See [land prices around Madikeri](/guides/land-prices-madikeri/).
