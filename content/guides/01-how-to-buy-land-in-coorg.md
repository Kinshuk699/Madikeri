Buying land in Coorg usually takes six steps: shortlist, visit, check the land records, agree terms in writing, register the sale deed at the Sub-Registrar's office, and get the land records changed into your name. Since 2020, anyone living in India can buy agricultural land in Karnataka, whether or not they farm.

## The steps

| Step | What happens | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shortlist areas and compare asking prices | Listings, [Land prices around Madikeri](/guides/land-prices-madikeri/) |
| 2 | Visit the land, ideally once in the monsoon | On site, [What to check when you visit](/guides/due-diligence-checklist-coorg/) |
| 3 | Check the land records (RTC, encumbrance certificate, survey sketch) | Bhoomi and Kaveri portals, with your lawyer |
| 4 | If you plan to build, check whether the land is converted | [Converting agricultural land in Kodagu](/guides/convert-agricultural-land-kodagu/) |
| 5 | Agree the price and terms in writing | With your lawyer |
| 6 | Pay stamp duty and register the sale deed | Sub-Registrar's office, [Stamp duty and registration](/guides/stamp-duty-registration-karnataka-land/) |
| 7 | Get the RTC updated to your name (mutation) | Taluk office |

## Visit in the rain if you can

Kodagu gets about 310 cm of rain a year, most of it between June and September. Roads, drainage and slopes look different in the monsoon, so a visit then tells you more than one in March.

## Ask for the records early

A serious seller will share the RTC, encumbrance certificate and survey sketch before you agree a price. If a seller won't, that tells you something.

## Building a house

Agricultural land has to be converted for non-agricultural use before you build on it. Some land is sold already converted; most land in Coorg isn't. Ask which it is at the start.

## FAQs

### Can a Bangalore professional buy land in Coorg?
Yes. Since 2020, anyone living in India can buy agricultural land in Karnataka without being a farmer. See [can a non-farmer buy agricultural land in Karnataka](/guides/non-farmer-buy-agricultural-land-karnataka/).

### Can an NRI buy land in Coorg?
Buying agricultural land as an NRI needs specific approval from the Reserve Bank of India. See [buying land in Coorg as an NRI](/guides/nri-buy-land-coorg/).

### Where is land registered in Coorg?
At the Sub-Registrar's office for the taluk. For Madikeri taluk, that's the Sub-Registrar Office in the Mini Vidhanasoudha, Madikeri.

### How long does the whole process take?
It depends mostly on how quickly the records are checked and the documents are ready. Registration itself is done in a day at the Sub-Registrar's office.
