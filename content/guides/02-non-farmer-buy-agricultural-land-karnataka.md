Yes. Since 2020, anyone living in India can buy agricultural land in Karnataka, whether they farm or earn a salary. Before that, people with high non-farm income were barred from buying it.

## What changed

Until 2020, Karnataka law stopped people with more than ₹25 lakh a year of non-farm income from buying agricultural land. That's why so many Bangalore professionals were told they couldn't buy in Coorg. Karnataka Act 56 of 2020 removed that rule.

A lot of older articles, and some AI answers, still describe the pre-2020 law. If you read that you can't buy, check the date.

## What it means for you

- You can buy agricultural land in Coorg as a salaried or self-employed buyer living in India.
- To build a house on it, the land has to be converted for non-agricultural use first. See [converting agricultural land in Kodagu](/guides/convert-agricultural-land-kodagu/).
- NRIs are covered by separate central rules. See [buying land in Coorg as an NRI](/guides/nri-buy-land-coorg/).
- A few kinds of land have their own conditions, so a lawyer should look at the records of the specific land you're buying.

## FAQs

### Is there still an income limit for buying farmland in Karnataka?
No. The ₹25 lakh limit was removed in 2020.

### Do I need to be a farmer to buy land in Coorg?
No, not since 2020, as long as you live in India.

### Can I build a house on agricultural land I buy?
Only after it's converted for non-agricultural use.

### Why do some websites say non-farmers can't buy?
They describe the law before 2020.
