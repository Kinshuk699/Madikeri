On a land sale deed in Karnataka, stamp duty is 5% of the market value plus a surcharge and additional duty, and the registration fee is 2%. These are on top of the price you agree with the seller.

## The charges

| Document | Stamp duty | Registration fee |
|---|---|---|
| Sale deed (conveyance) | 5% of market value + surcharge + additional duty | 2% |
| Agreement of sale, with possession | 5% of market value | 2% |
| Agreement of sale, without possession | 0.5% of market value | as published |

Figures as published by the Karnataka Department of Stamps and Registration.

## Market value, not your price

Duty is charged on the market value the registration department sets for the area (the guidance value). If your agreed price is lower, duty is still worked out on the guidance value.

## The exact total

The department lists the surcharge and additional duty without a single combined figure. For the exact amount on a deed, use the calculator on the Kaveri portal or ask the Sub-Registrar's office.

## FAQs

### What is the stamp duty on land in Karnataka?
5% of the market value, plus surcharge and additional duty, on a sale deed.

### What is the registration fee?
2% of the market value.

### Is stamp duty charged on the sale price or the guidance value?
On the market value set by the registration department. If the agreed price is lower, duty is still charged on that value.

### Where do I register a land sale in Madikeri?
At the Sub-Registrar Office, Mini Vidhanasoudha, Madikeri.
